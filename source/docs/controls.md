# Controls and physical interactions

[Back to the README](../README.md)

The names below use **Touch-style buttons**: X/Y on the left and A/B on the right, with **Motion** controls and **Move with: Left stick**. The right hand holds the melee weapon; the left aims the gun or handles items.

For your connected controllers and current settings, open **Show VR controls** on the in-game VR settings page. It has Gameplay, Menus and Gestures tabs. Older/custom layouts can change the bindings below.

## LEFT CONTROLLER

- **Stick:** move. **Click to run** until you centre the stick. While running forward, **press A or click the stick again to jump**; steering away from the run direction makes A dodge instead.
- **Trigger:** fire the gun or parry. Aim with your hand. When pointing at a quick-item card, take or use that item instead.
- **Grip:** open the quick-item arc. With body slots enabled, reach for a pouch and grip to take an item. Release to return an unused vial or throw a throwable.
- **X:** ready a Blood Vial, then bring it into your wrist or body to inject. Press again to put it away. With **Blood Vial: Button**, X heals directly.
- **Y:** open the quick-item arc. **On SteamVR's menu layout, Y opens the game's menu instead; hold it to recenter.**
- **Menu:** open the game's menu; hold to recenter when this button is available to the game. On SteamVR, it belongs to the SteamVR dashboard; use Y for the game menu.

## RIGHT CONTROLLER

- **Swing your hand:** attack with your melee weapon.
- **Trigger:** prepare a heavy attack; hold to charge, then swing.
- **Grip:** transform or prepare the weapon for a physical action, depending on the weapon (see below). Hold to show the two-hand grip guide when **Grip guide** is enabled.
- **Stick left/right:** turn using your chosen snap or smooth turning mode.
- **Stick down:** cycle to the next quick item. **Hold up:** make blood bullets.
- **Stick click:** target / lock on. Hold the click and push left/right to switch the corresponding hand's weapon; up makes blood bullets and down selects the next item.
- **A:** dodge. While running forward, press A to jump; steering away from the run direction makes A dodge instead.
- **B:** interact: talk, open doors, and pick up items.

## Rolling

While moving, press **A+B together**, or **B then A in quick succession**, to roll in the movement-stick direction. B-first accepts A within about 0.15 seconds. While locked on, the game quicksteps instead.

Standing still, tap A+B and release to roll backward. Holding the buttons for about 1.2 seconds while still enters grip adjustment instead. Release both buttons before trying another roll.

## Adjusting your weapon grip

Stand still and hold **A+B for about 1.2 seconds** until the weapon freezes. Line your controller up with it in the position you want, then release A or B to save. While adjusting, press the **right trigger** to restore that weapon's default grip. **Grip adjust** must be enabled.

## Physical evasion

**Lean, step or duck** to move the hunter out of a blow without a roll animation. This is physical evasion (a soft dodge). The separate lean-to-roll setting is off by default; when enabled, a quick sideways or backward lean triggers a dodge.

## Switching weapons and going unarmed

With **Shoulder weapon switch** enabled:

- **Right-hand weapon:** reach over your **right shoulder** and tap **B** to switch weapons.
- **Left-hand weapon:** reach over your **left shoulder** and tap **Y** to switch weapons.
- **Go unarmed:** keep the hand at its shoulder and **hold B or Y for about 0.7 seconds** to put away that hand's weapon.
- **Draw again:** while unarmed, tap the same shoulder button to draw your weapon back.

You can also switch weapons by holding the **right stick click** and pushing the stick left or right.

## Holding a weapon with both hands

For weapons with a shared two-hand handle:

1. **Squeeze the right grip** to free your left hand. With **Grip guide** enabled, hold the right grip to see where to grab.
2. Bring your **left hand to the weapon's handle**, then **squeeze the left grip**. Move into position before squeezing; sliding an already-squeezed hand onto the handle does not grab it.
3. Swing and steer with both hands. You can slide the support hand along the handle.
4. **Release both grip buttons** to let go with the support hand. Keeping either grip pressed keeps the attachment.

For the Hunter Axe, Kirkhammer, Ludwig's Holy Blade, Burial Blade, Chikage and Rifle Spear, the grab opens the two-hand form; letting go returns it to its one-hand form. The **Whirligig Saw** opens when the left hand grabs the handle, without the initial right-grip squeeze. While holding the transformed saw with both hands, hold either trigger to spin it.

Your left hand must be free: put away any item or readied Blood Vial first. Paired blades, bows, claws and fists use their own interactions instead of a shared handle.

## Splitting paired weapons / dual wielding

For **Blade of Mercy** and **Rakuyo**:

1. **Squeeze the right grip** to free your left hand.
2. Bring your **left hand next to the right hand at the hilt**, then **squeeze and hold the left grip**.
3. **Pull your hands apart** to split the weapon. You will feel feedback as it separates.
4. Attack with either hand's blade by swinging that hand.
5. To join them again, bring the blades together **side by side**, release the left grip if it is held, and **squeeze it again** to lock them together.

This splits supported paired weapons; it does not equip an arbitrary second right-hand weapon.

## Weapon-specific actions

These apply to Motion controls with physical weapon transformations enabled:

- **Bloodletter:** hold the right grip, turn the weapon toward yourself, and bring it into your character's chest to transform. Squeeze the right grip again to return it.
- **Tonitrus:** hold the right grip and raise the weapon overhead to charge it.
- **Boom Hammer:** hold the right grip until the furnace lights.
- **Beast Claw:** hold the right grip for two seconds to transform.
- **Simon's Bowblade:** hold the right grip for half a second to open the bow; squeeze again to close it. With the bow in your right hand, bring the arrow in your left hand to the string, squeeze the left trigger, pull back, and release the trigger to shoot. Take subsequent arrows from over your shoulder with the left trigger. Let the string down before releasing to cancel a shot.
- **Shield:** raise the shield in front of you to block.
- **Wrist-flick reload, when enabled:** after a shot, release the trigger and flick your left wrist firmly in any direction to reload.

## Physical gestures

You can perform recognised poses with your own head and hands. Your hunter stays under your control rather than playing a gesture animation. By default, the corresponding gesture must already be learned.

Stand close in front of the **Doll** and give the pose a moment to register:

- **Bow:** lower both hands and bow your head.
- **Point / wave:** extend one hand forward around shoulder height, with the other lowered.
- **Rally / approval:** raise one hand above your head and keep the other lowered.
- **Make Contact:** raise both hands above your head, apart from each other, and hold them still.

Physical recognition is limited to the Doll and the Make Contact encounter; it does not trigger every gesture everywhere. To open the normal gesture menu, **hold Y and press B** in the current motion layout.

## Items and interactions

- **Blood Vials:** press X to ready one, then bring it to your wrist or body to inject. Press X again to put it away. In seated mode, your lap also counts.
- **Body pouches:** with body slots enabled, reach to a pouch and squeeze the left grip to take its item. Release to return an unused vial or throw a throwable. Pouches follow the equipped quick-item slots.
- **Quick-item arc:** open it with the left grip (or Y when Y is not assigned to the game menu), then use the left trigger on an item card to take or use it.
- **Physical levers, when enabled:** grip a lever with either hand and pull.
- **Recenter:** face your intended forward direction and hold the game's menu button for about 0.7 seconds. Use Y on SteamVR's menu layout.
- **Change control style:** hold both stick clicks for one second to switch between Motion and Buttons controls.

**Buttons control style:** right trigger is a light attack; right grip is a heavy attack (hold to charge); left grip transforms; left trigger fires/parries. A dodges (hold to sprint), B interacts, X uses a vial, and Y uses the selected quick item. Click the left stick while sprinting to jump; hold the right stick click for lock-on.

**Move with: Right stick** swaps the sticks, stick clicks and face-button actions between hands. The weapon hands, triggers and grips stay on their original sides.
