Gehrman wheelchair - experimental, on by default (2026-11-04)

Set VR_GEHRMAN_CHAIR=0 before a launch to turn it off.
The current player's existing session is never modified by this environment setting.

Bare hands grip the chair anywhere on it (its back, sides, frame: 45 cm to 1.45 m up), push/pull
and steer; each grip is that hand's handle, and the hand is drawn there while it holds. Like a
real wheelchair it rolls along its axis and turns on its big wheels (beside the seat, 63 cm
ahead of the handles); they skid only when yanked hard. The hold steers it (the line between
two hands, or one hand's turn); rolling, it steers toward where the hold puts it; standing,
its back swings under hands moved sideways. A snap turn carries it with the hands. It keeps
pace with a run. Let go rolling and it rolls on at that pace (up to 3.5 m/s); let go turning
and it spins on briefly. Steps up to 30 cm are lifted at the leading edge, stairs are climbed
and descended (no step over 30 cm), ledges stop it. Catch either handle to take control.
Only travel while holding a handle counts toward the gift.
Native movement is capped and requires map clearance and ground support.
NPC conversation, menus, cinematics, stale tracking and late Gehrman story states deny interaction.
No quest flags or save structures are written directly.

Kos Lights is awarded through the native inventory API after
10 metres and at least 4 seconds of observed movement following accepted moves.
Owning the packet prevents duplicate grants. Progress so far survives menus, talks and
short breaks in the same session; it is not saved.
With VR_GEHRMAN_CHAIR=0 the earlier automatic grant applies instead.

Offline evidence: c8040 (entity 2100600) contains the wheelchair and four wheel bones;
boss Gehrman is c8050 (entity 2100800). The c8040 placement events end after placement.
ChrIns/EnemyIns vtable +0x5F8 resolves to 0x18CD600; code signatures gate installation.
Fixed state; no character-list scans or per-frame heap allocations in our controller.
An atomic nonblocking guard prevents concurrent or recursive native movement callbacks.

Release blockers:

- Verify wall/ledge collision, dialogue, rest/load, character switching and recenter behaviour.
