Edits: excerpts, filtering, fades, resampling and gain. gun_reload.pcm combines
clipload2 (0-.205 s), clipload1 (0-.250 s), and equipmentclicks (1.03-1.25 s)
at 0, .18 and .38 seconds. gun_empty.pcm uses the equipmentclicks excerpt.
Reproduce with scripts/prepare_reload_audio.py (Python + ffmpeg), with the three
source WAV files in Build/reload-sources. Output duration: .56 s / .22 s.
