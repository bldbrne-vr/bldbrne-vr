Rebuild with scripts/prepare_whip_audio.py and the original download.
The preparation script checks true peaks at 192 kHz. At runtime the clink loops back
to back while the chain reels in (0.48, scaled by recall speed; 5 ms on/12 ms off), and
closes the reel until the game's own close sound has been decoded from the player's
files (core/vr/game_sounds.h). Constant-power stereo pan, then the emulator/game output
volume. This small excerpt is embedded in the executable; no extra runtime download or audio device.
