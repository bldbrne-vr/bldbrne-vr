# Bldbrne-VR 0.9.0

- **A secret in the Hunter's Dream.** Something waits there for inquisitive and caring hunters to discover.
- **Blood Bullets, the hunter's way.** Use your right hand to pull an empty syringe from your left shoulder, then stab it into your arm to turn blood into bullets. It grabs, thumps and holds just like a Blood Vial.
- **Chalice Dungeon levers by hand.** Grab them and pull.
- **Feel what's hurting you.** Poison, frenzy and the rest creep in at the edges of your view as veins, each with its own look.
- **Hunter tools, meaner than ever.** Thrown Executioner's Gloves send their spirits after the nearest enemy, the Beast Roar spits embers, the Augur splatters, the Shaman Bone Blade whooshes and stabs. Better grips on every tool, and new sounds: a splash when you squeeze, a real gulp when you swallow.
- **Sharper weapons.** A new launcher option upscales your weapons' textures 2x.
- **Steady on the stairs.** Your weapon and shoulders stop bobbing when you climb.
- **Steady god rays.** The moonlight through the Hunter's Dream window and the blood moon's rays no longer swing around as you turn your head.
- **Combat tuning.** Two-handed swings hit a bit softer, swing speed counts a little more, you can swat bullets from a little farther, guns reload with a wrist flick, and more Strength means a harder pull on corpses.
- **Faster, smoother, lighter on memory.** A new memory system keeps the game smooth when your graphics card runs out of room, and the right eye keeps its own picture. Both eyes share their shadows, the renderer is built tuned to how it really runs, and finished frames reach your headset sooner.
- **Fixes**: blood-paint textures stay with water reflections off; shoulder vial pickup is more reliable; eyes too big for the headset are scaled to fit; new players on fast cards start on Light foveation, on 10 GB cards or less without water reflections, and on 8 GB or less with far shadows reduced. Foveation is a touch lighter at every level.

The release runtime uses ThinLTO with renderer PGO. Normal play keeps only startup and crash diagnostics.
