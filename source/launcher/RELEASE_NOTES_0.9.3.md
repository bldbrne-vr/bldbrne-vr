# Bldbrne-VR 0.9.3

- **The Amygdalan Arm, alive.** In whip form it's a living tentacle on the end of your arm. Swing and it lashes out to 3.5 m, then sags and pulls back in when you stop. Hold the trigger and it grabs whatever it hits: yank them off their feet, swing them round and hurl them. Press the trigger with your hand still and it stabs straight ahead. Every lash costs real stamina, so pick your moments.
- **Sharp where you look, faster everywhere else.** Foveated upscaling is now on by default: DLSS or FSR works the middle of each eye and a lighter pass handles the edges, for more frames. Choose Quality, Medium or Performance.
- **Doors, chests and lamps in your own hands.** Doors, gates, chests and coffins open without pulling you out of your eyes, and lamps light at once with their own sound. One setting: VR interactions.
- **No more slowdown in long sessions.** The frame rate no longer sinks after half an hour and a few areas: the renderer stopped waiting on the GPU for character motion data.
- **Stake Driver, kicked shut.** The charged blast slams the stake back into its normal form in the same instant, hit or miss. A harder kick up your arm, then a second jolt as it locks.
- **The Doll can reshape you.** Choose Rechannel Blood Echoes and move points between your attributes for free, at the same level. Each one can go as low as any origin starts it (Vitality 7, Endurance 8, Strength 9, Skill 9, Bloodtinge 5, Arcane 6).
- **A Call Beyond.** The gathered dark flares bright until its stars fly, with the game's own call.
- **Logarius' Wheel, spun up.** Each quick pull of the left trigger spins the wheel a step further and stacks a stronger arcane buff.
- **Boom Hammer.** A warm flicker of firelight when it's lit, and a truer pull-start.
- **Ladder skip on by default.** Long climbs go dark and you're there in seconds. Turn it off in You > Comfort.
- **Faster smooth turning.** Up to 240 degrees a second.
- **A new launcher.** A darker Victorian look, and the update button tells you which version is waiting.
- **Fixes**: the quick-item fan only comes back by itself after a quick put-back; the monocular's zoom limits; paired blades and Logarius' Wheel collide more cleanly.

The release runtime uses ThinLTO with renderer PGO, retrained for this version. Normal play keeps only startup and crash diagnostics.
