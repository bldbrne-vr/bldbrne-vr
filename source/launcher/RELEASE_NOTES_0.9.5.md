# Bldbrne-VR 0.9.5

- **The Kos Parasite, reborn.** With the Milkweed rune, swing to lash its tentacle. Transformed, each hand grows three tentacles that strike with your own swings. Pull the left trigger to spit poison where you look; transformed, hold it for the ritual, with its heartbeat and its vision. Without the rune, you punch.
- **Tonitrus calls the storm.** A full charge brings a bolt down from the sky: a flash over the whole world, thunder, and a jolt in your hands.
- **The Holy Moonlight Sword hits what you swing at.** After a full charge, the wave flies the moment your blade sweeps across where you're facing, and it finds the enemy in front of you. If a swing never crosses in front of you, let go of the trigger mid-swing to send it along the blade.
- **Split blades, sharper.** Split the Blade of Mercy or Rakuyo and each hand fights at its own pace. Rub the blades together four times to hone them: a ding, and each blade's next hit lands harder. Splitting takes a shorter pull too.
- **The Amygdalan Arm moves like a limb.** It coils before it cracks, stays out after a hard swing, and a hard chop from above slams it into the ground.
- **The bow, a little kinder.** An arrow you loose just beside an enemy curves onto it, and arrows hit 5% harder.
- **Charged kills send enemies flying,** every time.
- **Levers and doors.** Your hand slides down tall levers as you pull, levers out of reach keep their button prompt, and doors open sooner.
- **Weapon switches that land.** A B or Y press at your shoulder counts the moment you reach it, and one pressed mid-swing waits for the swing to end instead of getting lost.
- **Smoother menus.** The game holds your headset's refresh rate instead of dragging lower in menus.
- **Faster first start.** Preparing shaders after an update runs about twice as fast.
- **GPU Tweak III crash fixed.** ASUS GPU Tweak III's on-screen display could crash the game as it loaded; the game now keeps it out. If your GPU ever does crash, the game writes a report of what it was doing.
- **Fixes**: the death choice after a soft save always comes up live; chest items come out even when you clench your whole hand in a fight, and the chest pocket is easier to reach; the lean dodge is a little easier; the Beast Cutter no longer mistakes a turn for a circling swing; the Threaded Cane's chain no longer staggers you against a wall; the launcher saves its setup options as you change them.
- **Thanks** to Dirol, BlizzardNight and Chip for feedback, suggestions and bug reports.

The release runtime uses ThinLTO with renderer PGO, retrained for this version. Normal play keeps only startup and crash diagnostics.

Updating from 0.9.2 or older, the launcher's update button may call this version 0.9.150: that's 0.9.5. From 0.9.3 it shows 0.9.5.
