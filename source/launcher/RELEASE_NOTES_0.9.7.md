# Bldbrne-VR 0.9.7

- **The Chikage's scabbard is real.** Slide the blade into the scabbard at your hip, or hold the scabbard in your left hand, and the blade takes on blood. Snap it out fast for a quick draw: you dash forward, cut hard, and blood sprays off the steel. A killing draw can take a limb off.
- **Quick draws land.** The cut follows your swing, so a wide or wild draw still hits what it crosses, even several enemies.
- **Change graphics while you play.** In the VR menu, lamp shadows, lighting, moon glow and parallax now switch without a restart. Fog changes the next time you travel to another area.
- **Sharper lamp shadows.** Choose Low, Medium or High: more lamps cast crisp shadow edges near you, while staying fast. 12 GB cards start on Low, 16 GB cards on Medium.
- **The Bloodletter swings like a real flail.** It's heavy in one hand, and your left hand can take its hilt. Tap for its blood explosion, or hold the trigger and smash it down to set it off yourself. You feel the blood pulse in your hand as it builds, and the blast hits like a hammer.
- **Smoother SteamVR.** Your weapon hitches far less in your hand, most of all on a Quest through Link.
- **Fog you can fight through.** Shots cut a lane through it, explosions blow it away, and it drifts back slowly. Lamp glow in fog no longer builds into bright orbs.
- **Lean and duck dodges kick in sooner.** A quick lean protects you right away, and keeps protecting you if you keep leaning.
- **Whips cost stamina per lash.** The Threaded Cane's and the Beast Cutter's whip forms no longer swing for free.
- **Mods in the launcher.** Import Bloodborne mods and switch them on and off. Your game files stay untouched.
- **And fixes:** more enemies ragdoll on a kill, swatted bullets fly back to the shooter a bit more often, the death menu shows your soft save's time, the moon glow runs faster on 12 GB cards, poison and status flashes no longer drop out early, the Saif's transform rumble follows the blade, the Amygdalan Arm's club is a little heavier, and the Blood-starved Beast's church keeps the game's own fog.

The release runtime uses ThinLTO with renderer PGO. Normal play keeps only startup and crash diagnostics.

Updating from 0.9.2 or older, the launcher's update button may call this version 0.9.170: that's 0.9.7. From 0.9.3 it shows 0.9.7.
