# Bldbrne-VR 0.9.76

- **Left-handed, right on the Play page.** A Left-handed box now sits next to Play style: tick it and your trick weapon goes in your left hand, the gun in your right.
- **Settings say what "Game default" means.** Every setting now names the value it stands for, like "Game default (Off)" or "Game default (SMAA)".
- **Fewer floating orbs in The Old Hunters.** The glowing balls hanging over the corpse heaps in the Underground Corpse Pile and in the Research Hall's archway are gone. Real fires, candles and lamps keep their glow.

The release runtime uses ThinLTO with renderer PGO. Normal play keeps only startup and crash diagnostics.

Updating from 0.9.2 or older, the launcher's update button may call this version 0.9.176: that's 0.9.76. From 0.9.3 it shows 0.9.76.
