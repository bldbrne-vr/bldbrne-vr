# Bldbrne-VR 0.9.75

- **Your weapons talk.** The first time you take up a weapon with a trick of its own (the Chikage, the Rakuyo, the Boom Hammer, the Bloodletter and twelve more), a Yharnam voice tells you how to use it in VR, once. Switch weapons to cut it short.
- **Left-handed play.** Controls > Left-handed mirrors the whole world, so your main weapon is in your left hand and everything feels right. It works with motion controls in first person.
- **Dynamic resolution.** When a scene gets heavy, the picture drops a little sharpness instead of dropping frames, then climbs back when it can. It now works with foveated upscaling too. On by default; switch it off under Graphics > Performance.
- **Far fewer first-time stutters.** The game now prepares shaders for your own settings at startup, and covers many more places: Yahar'gul, Cainhurst, the Nightmare areas and The Old Hunters.
- **Free vials your way.** Choose how many Blood Vials you get when you run out (1 to 8, 3 by default) under Difficulty > Survival.
- **Tonitrus crackles blue-white** while it charges and glows faintly blue around you. An interrupted charge waits for a fresh lift.
- **Better throws and bows.** Molotovs leave your hand more reliably, thrown items get a gentle aim assist, and Simon's Bowblade arrows hit harder and fly a little further.
- **Easier Chikage.** Grabbing the scabbard and sliding the blade in at your hip are more forgiving.
- **Hits land harder.** Your controller buzzes a little longer and stronger when a swing connects.
- **And fixes:** large lamps cast shadows further on High, no more floating orbs at the Fishing Hamlet's poles, Laurence's wall candles or a Molotov's blast, a slightly longer muzzle flash, faster GPU copies, fewer glitches on textures the game draws itself, your headset's exact scaling and upscaler are kept, and the VR page's number rows snap to their steps.

The release runtime uses ThinLTO with renderer PGO. Normal play keeps only startup and crash diagnostics.

Updating from 0.9.2 or older, the launcher's update button may call this version 0.9.175: that's 0.9.75. From 0.9.3 it shows 0.9.75.
