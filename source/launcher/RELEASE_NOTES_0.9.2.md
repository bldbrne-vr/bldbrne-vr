# Bldbrne-VR 0.9.2

- **Smooth turning, smooth again.** Fixed a stutter that could start after travelling between lamps and stay until you restarted the game.
- **Logarius' Wheel, in both hands.** Free your left hand with the right grip, grab the wheel's far handle and it transforms right in your hands, no animation. Let go and it turns back. Three quick pulls of the left trigger rev it up and unleash its spin.
- **Pull-start the Boom Hammer.** Every right-grip squeeze is a pull: it sputters, catches and growls. Keep pumping, hold the grip, or knock its head with your other hand to light it. One good hit spends the fire, or squeeze again to put it out.
- **Skip ladder climbs.** A new option (off by default, in You > Comfort and the launcher): long ladders go dark while you climb at speed, and you're back at the top or bottom in seconds.
- **Heavier cannon.** Bigger kick and punchier vibrations, and the kick no longer counts as a reload flick.
- **Executioner's Gloves, your way.** Gather them to the full, then open your hand to throw.
- **More frames for the headset.** The desktop window now updates at a steady 30 fps, leaving a little more of your graphics card for VR.
- **Fixes**: a quick Back out of a menu no longer drinks a Blood Vial; the right eye no longer keeps stale patches in rare scenes; a seat you can ride no longer pushes into walls; a broken game text file can no longer hang the game.

The release runtime uses ThinLTO with renderer PGO, retrained for this version. Normal play keeps only startup and crash diagnostics.
