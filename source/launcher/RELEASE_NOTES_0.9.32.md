# Bldbrne-VR 0.9.32

- **VR menus hold steady on Virtual Desktop.** The VR settings page and the lamp menu no longer tilt and sway the world with your head under Virtual Desktop (VDXR). Both are now drawn into the game image itself, which also keeps them lighter on Link.
- **The Brain of Mensis lever.** It uses the game's own prompt again: walk up and press the button to pull it. Every other lever is still pulled by hand.
- **The Amygdalan Arm snaps back cleaner.** In whip form, a small swing now pulls the tentacle straight back in instead of leaving it hanging out and flailing. Only a big swing lets it hang for a moment before it comes back.
- **Dodging in the controls guide.** It now explains how to dodge with your body: lean, step aside or duck under a swing. In standing Motion controls a quick lean just before a hit can make you briefly invulnerable.

The release runtime uses ThinLTO with renderer PGO. Normal play keeps only startup and crash diagnostics.
