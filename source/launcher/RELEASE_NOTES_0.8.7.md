# Bldbrne-VR 0.8.7

- **Tiny Tonitrus, fixed and loud.** Hold the trigger until it crackles, then strike down. The lightning goes where you look, or at the enemy you're locked on to, and lifting it to wind up no longer fires it behind you. A brand-new bolt too: forked, white-hot, with restrikes and a thunder crack in your hand. And it's a bit bigger in your fist.
- **New hunter tools in your hand.** The Augur of Ebrietas, Beast Roar, A Call Beyond and the Executioner's Gloves all have new animated models. Crush the Augur and its tentacles shoot from your fist a beat later (or eat it and they burst out of you). Hold Beast Roar and the claw clenches and shakes, then bursts open with a roar. Toss the Gloves to call the spirits where they land, and stab an enemy with the Shaman Bone Blade to turn it. No hold rings on these: the animation tells you when it's ready.
- **Grab the dead.** Go unarmed and your fingers follow your controller. Push, lift, drag and carry corpses with your bare hands. Charged Stake Driver kills send bodies flying.
- **Send bullets home.** Swing into a shot and it's more likely to fly back at the shooter. A still blade just blocks, and that costs stamina.
- **Your pouches stay put.** Pouches, body slots and the shoulder vial no longer vanish after you look away. Panic vial grabs mid-fight are easier, and the waist pocket stops buzzing while you run.
- **PS VR2 vibration with feeling.** With PSVR2 Toolkit you get crisp ticks, deep thumps and rolling thunder instead of one flat buzz.
- **Smoother play.** Much less stutter on your first hits (blood and spark shaders now get built when the game starts), a much faster start with a big shader cache, a smooth Monocular again, a few percent more speed in VR, and fewer drops on 6-8 GB graphics cards.
- **A better launcher.** A searchable guide to every VR move, a tab for each controller with its own button names, The Old Hunters found by itself, and Y for menus on Virtual Desktop.
- **Also**: going unarmed at the shoulder is quicker; X can open the fan or ready a vial; flat mode is faster; no cut-off buttons at 150% scaling; no false "permission denied" at autosave.
