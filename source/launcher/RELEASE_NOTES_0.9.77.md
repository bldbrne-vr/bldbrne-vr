# Bldbrne-VR 0.9.77

- **No more hat in your face.** Every hat, hood and helmet is now hidden in first person, drapes and scarves too, so nothing clips into your view: the Mensis Cage, Gascoigne's Cap, the Black Hooded Iron Helm and the rest. Your shadow still wears it.
- **Cleaner sound.** The game now keeps a little sound ready ahead, so the crackles and pops when it ran dry are gone.
- **The Blacksky Eye shows where it lands.** Hold it up and a faint mark shows where its meteor will hit. Shake it and see what happens.
- **A heavier Tonitrus.** More weight in the head, and its iron ball carries on after a swing.
- **Easier to grab.** Levers reach a little further, the two-handed grip is a touch easier to take, a fan card forgives a small slip of your grip, and the items on your chest and waist sit on your armor.
- **The item fan stays closed** after you put an item back, use it or throw it. Open it again for the next one.
- **Smoother Chikage and Saif.** Sliding the Chikage home is more forgiving along the last of the blade, its draw flares a little less and dismembers a kill more often (58%), and the Saif's blade clicks into place on the right frame.
- **Fog that lingers.** Gunshots and explosions keep the fog parted a little longer, and it drifts back a little slower.
- **Quicker ladders.** The fast climb kicks in a touch sooner.
- **And fixes:** 640 MB less memory reserved at start, less work on draws that need no VR effects, the Perf Scan measures with your own picture settings and shows what Apply changed, and the low-stamina grey starts later and fades in more gently.

The release runtime uses ThinLTO with renderer PGO. Normal play keeps only startup and crash diagnostics.

Updating from 0.9.2 or older, the launcher's update button may call this version 0.9.177: that's 0.9.77. From 0.9.3 it shows 0.9.77.
